Wisp 2.4 Lap Delta Test 1

Enable Appearance > Gauges > Lap Delta > Show lap delta.
Choose Session best or Previous lap. Complete a full circuit lap to set the reference.
Negative seconds mean ahead; positive seconds mean behind.
The bar fills left or right up to two seconds; the number shows the full difference.
Use Edit HUD layout to move the gauge. Size, bar visibility and colors are in the same panel.
Enable Show live track map for a separate movable map with your live car position.
The first full lap builds the circuit outline; later laps keep the full circuit visible.
Map size adjusts from 50% to 300%. Track, car marker and background colors are independent.
Reset reference laps starts a new comparison. Rewinds, race restarts, telemetry interruptions
and car changes reset the session. Profiles retain the delta and map options.

This private build is for checking lap timing and track-position matching in FH6.
It keeps Wisp 2.4's existing native HUD and G-SYNC presentation.
