Wisp 2.6.3 Compatibility and support test
Private build ID: adaptive-support-20261005-03

This is a private test build, not a published release. It updates your existing
Wisp installation and keeps settings, profiles, calibrations, saved runs and
the Clips library. It is not a separate installation.

Changes in this build
- A dismissible note from Views appears when opening Wisp, at most once per
  24 hours. It stays open until you press its X. Its GitHub repo button opens
  Views2k/Wisp so you can leave a star without dismissing the note.
- Wisp can recognize unchanged HUD and Tune readers after a Forza update on
  both Steam and Xbox PC. A changed version number alone no longer blocks them.
- Game identity, reader structure, current-car ownership and value checks remain
  required. If an optional reader changes, the verified features stay available.
- Temporary read failures can be retried without restarting Wisp.

Suggested checks
1. Start your usual Steam or Xbox PC version of Forza, then Wisp. Drive into the
   open world and check the HUD against the car and the game's displayed values.
2. Open Tune and refresh. Compare the displayed values with your current tune.
3. Switch cars and confirm the HUD and Tune show the new car's values.
4. Close and reopen Forza with Wisp still running. Confirm Wisp reconnects.
5. Save and play a short clip with your existing recording settings.

If a read fails, use Copy details on Tune or export a debug ZIP from Diagnostics.
Include the private build ID above with your report. This build does not enable
per-frame diagnostic logging or change your recording settings.

Steam and Xbox read-only comparisons passed on the October 5 game builds.
Unknown future builds still need to pass the same validation; changed game
readers, inaccessible memory or ambiguous matches must be rejected.
