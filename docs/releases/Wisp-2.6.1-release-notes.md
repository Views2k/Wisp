# Wisp 2.6.1

[Download Wisp 2.6.1 for Windows](https://github.com/Views2k/Wisp/releases/download/v2.6.1/Wisp-Setup-2.6.1.exe)

## Clips recovery hotfix

Fixes the shutdown error that could leave clipping unavailable after closing Forza, requiring a Wisp restart even after the recorder had exited.

Wisp now retries delayed recorder shutdown automatically and resumes clipping when Forza is ready. If you turn clipping off during recovery, it stays off.
