# Wisp 2.6.11

[Download the latest Wisp release for Windows](https://github.com/Views2k/Wisp/releases/latest)

## Chat with Views

- Open your conversation from Extras to read and reply in a compact chat popup.
- Follow the conversation in order, with timestamps, delivery status and earlier messages.
- Keep your draft when reopening chat and retry a message safely if delivery is interrupted.
- Attach recent diagnostics after reviewing them to help investigate a problem.

## Clearer updates

- Available updates take priority on the dashboard, with the update message and Update action kept together.
- Announcements return when eligible and retain your dismissal choice and their expiry.
- Improve update progress reporting through the restart into the new version.
