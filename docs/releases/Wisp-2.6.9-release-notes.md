# Wisp 2.6.9

[Download the latest published Wisp release for Windows](https://github.com/Views2k/Wisp/releases/latest)

## Automatic updates

- Wisp checks for updates when it opens and every 15 minutes while running.
- Updates install and restart Wisp automatically after Forza closes and your current recording, export or unsaved run is finished.
- Downloads are verified before installation, and completed setup and saved settings carry over.

## Boost gauge up to 200 PSI

- Set **Maximum boost** from **20 to 200 PSI** in 10 PSI steps under **Appearance → Gauges → Boost gauge**.
- The gauge scale, numbered labels and tick marks adjust together for PSI and bar.
- Your chosen maximum saves with settings and HUD profiles.

## News and compatibility

- News and announcements refresh about every 30 seconds while Wisp is running.
- Improved Forza build and storefront identification makes compatibility reports more precise.
- Temporary feature pauses can target a specific Forza build or graphics-hardware group and end automatically.
