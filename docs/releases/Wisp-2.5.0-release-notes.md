# Wisp 2.5

Wisp 2.5 adds two beta features for circuit driving.

**Lap delta (beta)** shows how far ahead or behind your session best or previous lap you are, compared at the same place on the track. A negative delta means ahead; a positive delta means behind. An optional bar shows up to two seconds either way.

- Race and Rivals laps use the game's own lap clocks.
- Time Attack laps at Legend Island, Hokubu, Soni and Sekibe are timed from start-line crossings, because the game does not send Time Attack lap times.
- Your lap continues through pauses and rewinds. A reset, restart or abandoned Time Attack attempt starts a fresh lap.

**Live track map (beta)** follows your car around an enlarged outline of the circuit, built from your first full lap.

Turn both on in Appearance under Lap delta. Colors are in Colors, and Edit HUD layout moves either overlay. A banner on the Dashboard introduces these features once.
