# Wisp 2.3.4

Wisp 2.3.4 fixes the app accent-color crash, supplementary-gauge attachment and update recovery. It also includes optional **beta shift guidance**, off by default.

- Change the app accent color safely while the dashboard is on an inactive tab. The dashboard oval effect returns in the selected color when you switch back.
- Keep power and torque gauges separate in Minimal, Combined, Box and Native Digital, preserving attachment preferences for Native Analogue. Include the boost and tire-temperature preview fixes from 2.3.2.
- Retain update-helper repair instructions, keep Wisp open when an update cannot start, and ask you to exit a running Wisp copy before installation.

**Shift guidance is beta.** Calibrate each supported combustion car and tune with a rolling full-throttle pull and a confirming upshift. Wisp compares measured output in adjacent gears and shows configurable green, yellow and flashing red stages around the gear number. Missing RPM coverage or a changed configuration requires calibration before guidance is available.

The beta currently requires Forza Horizon 6 Steam build **6.440.853.0** and excludes EVs. Shift targets are still being validated. Red marks a calibrated RPM target, not a prediction of driver reaction or shift recovery; ideal timing across every car, tune and racing condition has not been verified.

The full installer includes the update helper and preserves settings, profiles, tire calibration and saved runs.
