# Wisp 2.6.10

[Download the latest Wisp release for Windows](https://github.com/Views2k/Wisp/releases/latest)

## Tune performance

- View braking distance, lateral G, acceleration, top speed, mechanical balance, aero balance and aero efficiency alongside your tune.
- Open Forza's Tune screen, wait for its performance results, then select **Refresh** in Wisp and choose **Performance**.
- Save the results with your tune and compare them side by side, with differences shown for each measurement.

## Clips recovery

- Clips pauses after three failed recording attempts and displays a message. Re-enable clipping when you're ready to try again.
- Normal resolution changes remain separate from recording failures.
- Copied error details include the graphics adapter selected for capture and encoding.
