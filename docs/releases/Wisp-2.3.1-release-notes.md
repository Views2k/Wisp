# Wisp 2.3.1

Boost and tire-temperature gauges now work as separate overlays with Minimal, Combined and Box layouts.

- Enable either gauge in **Appearance > Gauges**, then use **Edit HUD layout** to place it beside Wisp or the game's own speedometer.
- Attachment applies only to compatible Native layouts. Other layouts keep these gauges separate, even when your saved Native attachment setting is on. Switching back restores that preference.
- The Native Digital boost rail stays unchanged. The analogue boost gauge remains a separate overlay beside a digital speedometer.
- Preserve gauge artwork, sizing, colors, smoothing, saved positions, profiles and recordings.
