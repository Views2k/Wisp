# Wisp 2.6.2

[Download Wisp 2.6.2 for Windows](https://github.com/Views2k/Wisp/releases/download/v2.6.2/Wisp-Setup-2.6.2.exe)

## Fixes and improvements

- Saved clips now appear first on the Clips page. Recording settings are folded below your clips.
- Rename and delete clips in Wisp: right-click a clip, or select it and press F2 or Delete. Deleting asks first.
- Search saved clips by name, date, resolution, frame rate, HDR or lossless.
- Exporting an HDR or lossless clip now explains the compatible copy and the original recording before the save window opens.
- Fixed a white outline appearing on options and buttons after clicking them. Outlines now appear only when navigating with the keyboard.
- When you switch to Wisp, it now releases a mouse pointer confined by a game and shows Wisp's pointer right away.
